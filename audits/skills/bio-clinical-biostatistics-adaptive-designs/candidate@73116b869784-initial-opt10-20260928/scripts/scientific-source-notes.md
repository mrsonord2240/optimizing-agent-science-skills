# Scientific and regulatory source notes

Checked on 2026-09-28 against authoritative public sources.

- FDA still labels *Adaptive Design Clinical Trials for Drugs and Biologics* as Final Level 1 Guidance, December 2019.
- FDA still labels the oncology-specific *Master Protocols: Efficient Clinical Trial Design Strategies to Expedite Development of Oncology Drugs and Biologics* as Final Level 1 Guidance, March 2022.
- FDA published a separate, broader *Master Protocols for Drug and Biological Product Development* revised draft in June 2026. The candidate's oncology-specific 2022 statement is not false, but its regulatory-landscape reference is incomplete for current general master-protocol work.
- FDA labels ICH E20 *Adaptive Designs for Clinical Trials* as Draft Level 1 Guidance, September 2025. The candidate correctly says the ICH text is not final, but its June 2025 milestone wording should be distinguished from the later FDA issue date.
- FDA labels *Use of Bayesian Methodology in Clinical Trials of Drug and Biological Products* as draft guidance dated January 2026.
- FDA lists the BOIN statistical method as Fit-for-Purpose, issued December 10, 2021. This supports a determination for the method, not a claim that FDA generally "prefers" BOIN.
- FDA lists the oncology dosage-optimisation guidance as final, August 2024.

The tooling access record is retained at `evidence/regulatory-access.tsv`. This audit adjudicated only the status claims needed for the candidate review; it did not treat HTTP availability as scientific validation.
