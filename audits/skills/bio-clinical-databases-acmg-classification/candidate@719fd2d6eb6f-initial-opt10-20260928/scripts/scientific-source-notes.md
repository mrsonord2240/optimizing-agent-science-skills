# Scientific source notes

- Brnich et al. 2020 Table 3 maps OddsPath `<0.053` to BS3, `<0.23` to BS3 Moderate, `<0.48` to BS3 Supporting, `0.48–2.1` to indeterminate, `>2.1` to PS3 Supporting, `>4.3` to PS3 Moderate, `>18.7` to PS3 Strong, and `>350` to PS3 Very Strong: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6938631/>.
- Abou Tayoun et al. 2018 requires a biologically relevant transcript/exon, LoF disease mechanism, NMD assessment, and critical-region/size review; PVS1 and PM4 must not be double-counted: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6185798/>.
- ClinGen SVI splicing guidance further requires transcript-consequence and rescue-transcript review instead of automatically treating every canonical splice result as a fixed PVS1 strength: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10357475/>.
- Bergquist et al. 2025 reports calibrated AlphaMissense evidence reaching pathogenic Strong and benign Moderate; the published intervals place 0.170–0.791 in the indeterminate band, not at the candidate's 0.2/0.7 supporting calls: <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40084623/>.
- Tavtigian et al. 2020 supports the 8/4/2/1 signed point system and classification boundaries used in the candidate: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8011844/>.
- Current GeneBe endpoint documentation requires `chr`, `pos`, `ref`, `alt`, and `genome` for the single-variant GET: <https://docs.genebe.net/docs/api/endpoints/>.
- The current CSpec REST documentation describes `/cspec/srvc`, identity lookup, and versioned gene/specification endpoints: <https://genboree.org/gitlab/clingen/cspec/cspec-registry/-/wikis/CSpec-Registry-API>.
